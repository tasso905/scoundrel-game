          How to play Scoundrel

          Fight your way through a 44-card dungeon. Clear every card to win; reach 0 health and you're slain.

          The cards

          • ♠♣
            Monsters — all clubs and spades. Value 2–10, J 11, Q 12, K 13, A 14.
          • ♦
            Weapons — diamonds 2–10. Reduce monster damage by their value.
          • ♥
            Potions — hearts 2–10. Heal by their value, up to 20.

          Each room

          Four cards are dealt face up. Choose one:

          • Face it — resolve any 3 of the 4 cards, in any order. The 4th card stays and becomes the first card of the next room.
          • Avoid it — slide all 4 cards to the bottom of the dungeon. You can't avoid two rooms in a row, and once you resolve a card you're committed.

          Weapons

          Picking up a weapon equips it right away. Your old weapon, and every monster stacked on it, is discarded.

          Fighting with a weapon, you take monster − weapon damage (never below 0), and the monster is stacked on the weapon.

          The weapon only goes down

          After a kill, the weapon can only be used on monsters of equal or lower value than the last one it slew.

          1. Equip ♦5, then fight ♠J (11) → take 6. Last defeated: 11.
          2. Fight ♣6 → 6 ≤ 11, so take 1. Last defeated: 6.
          3. ♠8 appears → 8 > 6, so ♦5 can't be used. Bare-handed: take 8.

          Tip: you can always choose to fight bare-handed to keep a weapon's chain high for a bigger monster later.

          Potions

          One potion per room

          Only the first potion you use in a room heals. Any other potion in that room is discarded with no effect.

          At 14 health with ♥5 and ♥9 in the room: drink ♥9 to reach 20 (the extra 3 is lost). The ♥5 does nothing if used in this room, so it's often better to leave it as your carried card.

          Scoring

          • Win: score = your remaining health.
          • Lose: score = minus the total value of monsters still in the dungeon and room.
